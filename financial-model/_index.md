The Financial Model covers the financial engine behind the Direct-Hold Solutions: the Narrow
Banking Model, under which buildings are intended to be built with zero construction debt and
borrowing comes only after buildings are leased. Articles here describe the fees Woodfine
charges and earns, and how its escrowed stake is designed to keep it aligned with investors.

Content is structural — how the financing discipline is designed to work — not a projection of
any particular building's economics or a guarantee of any financial outcome.
