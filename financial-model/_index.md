Under the Narrow Banking Model, buildings are intended to be built with equity and no
construction debt, and borrowing is intended to begin only once a building is leased. The
Financial Model covers that financing discipline, the compensation structure designed to
keep the developer's capital at risk alongside the investor's, and the methodology used to
value and forecast. Content is structural — how the model is designed to work — not a
projection of any particular building's economics or a guarantee of any financial outcome.
