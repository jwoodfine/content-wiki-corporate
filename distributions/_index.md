Distributions and Transfers covers the distribution policy and the unit-transfer mechanism.
Distributions are targeted to be paid from Distributable Income when and if declared — they are
never guaranteed. Perpetual Equity is the mechanism intended to allow unitholders to offer their
units for sale at a time of their choosing — no forced exit and no redemption window by design.

Content here describes the policy and the mechanism; it does not promise a buyer, a price, or
any particular distribution outcome.
