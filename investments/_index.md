The Investment Vehicles covers the investment vehicles, present and planned: the parent
company's Common Shares and the four Direct-Hold Solutions planned for Canada, the United
States, Spain, and Mexico. Articles here describe what each vehicle is, its legal form, and the
eligibility framework that applies in each investor's home jurisdiction.

Content is structural — what each vehicle is and how it is organised — not the terms of any
specific offering or the projected outcomes of any particular asset.
