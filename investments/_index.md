An investor in a Direct-Hold Solution holds equity in one named building, not a share of a
commingled pool. The Investment Vehicles covers what that structure is, the legal form it
takes in each of the four jurisdictions, and the exemptions under which units are
distributed. Articles here are structural: what each vehicle is and how it is organised,
not the terms of any specific offering or the projected outcome of any particular asset.
